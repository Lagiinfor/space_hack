🧪 What is it made of?

This is the object's spectrum: its brightness at each infrared colour. Each dot is one SPHEREx photo; each line is one sky pass. Dips in the shaded strips mean molecules (methane, water ice, CO₂, CO) are absorbing light. If the lines don't match, the object changed.

➜ Things that moved

Found automatically: every dot is tracked across the sky passes, and anything that moved steadily is checked against ESA's Gaia catalogue. Known = Gaia predicts it. Candidate = nothing explains it, so it is worth a closer look.
